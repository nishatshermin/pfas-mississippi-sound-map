Click a river/stream sample to show every stream that could drain to it; click an empty spot to clear.

Turn the lens on, then drag or zoom the map to move samples under it. Click a flag below the lens to open or close its chart.

Coast bars: the river samples whose rivers reach the sea along each stretch of coast. Length = upstream stream length; color = average of the chosen PFAS measure. Zoom in for finer stretches; click a bar to show its streams and samples.

Bar length: upstream stream length (log scale; dashed lines at 10, 100, 1,000 km and so on). Turn on the coast bars to see the current stretch size.
